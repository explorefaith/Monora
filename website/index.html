Four ways to start listening:

Bengali Hindi English Trending

Each chip searches official Deezer charts and plays 30‑second previews — marked with a tiny 30s tag, so you always know what you're hearing.

Your library, first

Songs, albums and artists straight from your phone's storage. No account, no upload — nothing ever leaves your device.

Discover online

Bengali, Hindi and English picks from official public APIs with 30‑second previews. No scraping, no shady sources.

Keep what you love

Favorites and your last queue stay on your phone. Stop mid‑song, come back tomorrow — Monora remembers exactly where you left off.

Warm Vanilla Cream

The interface is soft on purpose: two‑layer shadows instead of borders, presses that squish and spring back like icing, and a serif that renders Bangla beautifully. These are the exact colors it's built from.
